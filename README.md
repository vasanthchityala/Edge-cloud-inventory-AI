\# Edge-Cloud Inventory Intelligence Platform



An AI-powered multi-store inventory intelligence platform for demand forecasting, inventory risk detection, edge-cloud intelligence, and intelligent inventory rebalancing.



\## Overview



The platform combines predictive demand forecasting with inventory risk analysis and edge-cloud coordination to support intelligent inventory management.



\### Key Features



\- Demand forecasting

\- Inventory risk detection

\- Edge/store-level intelligence

\- Cloud-level aggregation

\- Inter-store transfer optimization

\- Unified priority scoring

\- Transfer action validation

\- Interactive management dashboard
